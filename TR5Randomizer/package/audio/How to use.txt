Copy your HQ audio files from the TR4-6 Remasters into the folders of
the languages you want to use with the ingame language switch.

JP is still WIP.

The sound effects work the same way. Copy MAIN.SFX out of the TR4-6
Remaster - it sits in 5\DATA - into this folder and keep the name. The
game then plays its sound effects at 44.1 kHz instead of the original
22 kHz. Without the file nothing changes.
