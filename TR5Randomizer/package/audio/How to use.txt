Copy your HQ audio files from your own TR4-6 Remaster installation
into the folders of the languages you want to use with the ingame
language switch. Nothing of the Remaster is included here.

JP is still WIP.

The sound effects need no copy: with your own TR4-6 Remaster installed,
the game reads its MAIN.SFX (it sits there in 5\DATA) where it lies
and plays its sound effects at 44.1 kHz instead of the original
22 kHz. A MAIN.SFX in this folder, under that name, is used before
it. With neither, the game plays the original samples.
