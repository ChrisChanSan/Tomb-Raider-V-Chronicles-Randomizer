Copy the loading screens from the TR4-6 Remasters (they sit in 5\PIX\HD)
into the HD folder:
ANDREA1.DDS to RICH3.DDS, TITLE.DDS and LOGO_DE / LOGO_EU / LOGO_FR /
LOGO_US.DDS (1920 x 1080, BC7).

With them in place the game shows them as loading screens, builds the
logo screen from TITLE plus the LOGO of your language, and draws that
logo in the title menu. Security Breach borrows RICH3.DDS. Without the
files everything stays as in the original. The logo follows the
language set in the options after the next restart.
