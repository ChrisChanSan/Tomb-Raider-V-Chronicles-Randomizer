Which enemies may be swapped for which, and which may not.

Re-measured against the source on 2026-08-30. The original survey is from
2026-08-02; everything the TR3 port, the cross pool and the balance file
changed since then has been corrected here, and the entries that were right
are unchanged.

The categories below are about TRIGGER WIRING, not difficulty. Whether an
enemy may stand somewhere is decided by the pool tables in
TR5Randomizer\src\core\Randomizer.cpp (kCross, kSpaceNeeds, kHerds,
kSpawnOcb) and by the per-level lists in Preparation\Tools\ZoneAnalysis.ps1.
This document explains WHY those tables look the way they do.


================================================================
Pool B - freely swappable
================================================================
Never calls GetAITarget(), ignores AIPatrol1/AIAmbush markers entirely, and
always aims straight at Lara through the generic mood system.

	1 DOG (dog.cpp)
	2 LION (lion.cpp)
	3 CROW (crow.cpp) - see "the four TR3 guests" below: slot 43 is the
	  vulture now. The control is still CrowControl and still Pool B.
	4 ROMAN_GOD (romangod.cpp) - only checks ai_bits & GUARD for a random
	  attack roll, no real marker use
	5 ROMAN_GOD2 (romangod.cpp) - engine slot 461, added by this project:
	  the hammer statue from Andrea3, so the sword and hammer versions can
	  stand in one level. Same control, same rules.
	6 AUTOGUN (autogun.cpp) - stationary gun, aims via LOS(), not
	  "intelligent"
	7 GUNSHIP (tower.cpp) - scripted helicopter, position-based, not
	  "intelligent"


================================================================
Pool A - wired to markers
================================================================
Calls GetAITarget(), looks for AIPatrol1/AIAmbush markers when the matching
ai_bits are set, and fires triggers on ambush. Only replace with another
Pool A enemy when the spot has patrol/ambush markers in range.

	1 The soldier family, all on InitialiseSas/SasControl (sas.cpp):
	  BLUE_GUARD, SWAT, SWAT_PLUS, MAFIA, SCIENTIST, CRANE_GUY, BLUE_GUARD2
	  CORRECTION 2026-08-30: this line used to begin with SAS and end with
	  SAILOR. Both left the family when the TR3 guests took their slots.
	  BLUE_GUARD2 (engine slot 460, added by this project) joined it: the
	  joby guard model, so both guard models can exist in one level. It is
	  also the animation anchor the rest of the family falls back to, and
	  the chef borrows its death - see MODIFICATIONS.md.
	2 MAFIA2 (mafia2.cpp)
	3 PIERRE, LARSON (pierre.cpp)
	4 HITMAN (hitman.cpp)
	5 HUSKIE (huskie.cpp) - slot 85, the husky. Not to be confused with
	  DOG (41); upstream calls both "Dog".
	6 IMP (imp.cpp)
	7 ATTACK_SUB (minisub.cpp)
	8 SNIPER (sniper.cpp)
	9 GREEN_TEETH (swampy.cpp)
	10 SKELETON (skeleton.cpp)
	11 TWOGUN (twogun.cpp)
	12 WILLOWISP, MAZE_MONSTER (willwisp.cpp)
	13 GLADIATOR (gladiatr.cpp)
	14 CHEF (chef.cpp) - moved here from the special cases on 2026-08-30,
	  see below.

	REAPER used to stand in this list as "minisub.cpp, JellyControl". Slot
	75 is the raptor now. JellyControl still exists in minisub.cpp but is
	assigned to no object at all - the jellyfish is gone from the game.

	TWOGUN, a note that is still true: he appears in the same deltapak.cpp
	cutscene logic as the chef (stealth3_start/end), but TwogunControl has
	a perfectly normal hit_points <= 0 check with its own death animation.
	The cutscene is an optional stealth-kill bonus for him, not a
	requirement. He was also once immune to normal weapon damage through
	obj->undead = 1; that was removed and confirmed in game.
	CORRECTION 2026-08-30: his health is 90, not the 60 this document gave
	- raised again after the cross-mix playtest of 2026-08-15. And it is no
	longer a constant to look up here: since #108 every enemy's health
	comes from Balance.txt, where HP_TwoGun ships with 90.


================================================================
The four TR3 guests
================================================================
Added by this project (#88, #97, #99, #103). They occupy four object slots
whose vanilla contents TR5 never used, so nothing was displaced:

	SAS    (31) -> T-Rex,        InitialiseCreature / DinoControl
	CROW   (43) -> Vulture,      InitialiseCrow / CrowControl
	SAILOR (53) -> Compsognathus, InitialiseCompy / CompyControl
	REAPER (75) -> Raptor,       InitialiseCreature / RaptorControl

Read the slot NUMBER, not the name, whenever you look at one of these in
trview or in a level dump - the enum name still says SAS while the model is
a dinosaur. The project ships its own trview name table for exactly this
(Preparation\Item Infos\types.json).

Their models and sounds are not in this repository; the randomizer reads
them from the player's own Tomb Raider III installation at run time. Which
files, and their checksums, are in Preparation\Tr3Checksums.csv.

The Compsognathus is the only enemy the pool moves as a GROUP: kHerds gives
type 53 a count of 4, because one Compy alone is not the animal.


================================================================
Special cases - own rules
================================================================

	1 Soldier Statue (ROMAN_GOD, type 61) - stands visibly on the floor,
	  but on waking is moved 512 units back and 1024 units up, plays a
	  stand-up animation and only then lands on the tile it was placed on.
	  The position visible in the level is NOT the combat position: the
	  target spot needs room for that shift and the landing animation.
	  The pool gives it OCB 1 outside Andrea3 (kSpawnOcb) so it behaves as
	  the sword version; the hammer version (461) gets OCB 0.

	2 Guardian (guardian.cpp) - EXCLUDED FROM THE POOL, still, and this has
	  not changed since 2026-08-02. The actual enemy is only the head with
	  its two eyes; the cage parts around it are separate ANIMATING objects.
	  InitialiseGuardian links them by searching the ENTIRE item list for
	  the first object of a matching type - ANIMATING12 for the centre,
	  ANIMATING12_MIP with an exactly matching y_rot in 45-degree steps for
	  the eight segments, PUZZLE_ITEM4 for a hidden part - with no error
	  handling when nothing matches. That works in vanilla only because
	  there is exactly one matching set per level. Two guardians in one
	  level, a changed item order, or a move into a level without the
	  companion objects, and the head attaches to the wrong parts or none.
	  It appears in no pool table in Randomizer.cpp.

	3 Hydra (hydra.cpp, type 63) - three items, three separate healths, one
	  creature. All three sit on EXACTLY the same editor position; the
	  visible spread comes from the OCB (trigger_flags), which here encodes
	  the chain position instead of a pickup style:
	    OCB 0 = centre, OCB 1 = Z += 384, OCB 2 = Z -= 384
	    (always additionally X -= 256, y_rot fixed at 0x4000, and the start
	    frame offset by 30*OCB so the heads do not move in lockstep)

	  CORRECTION 2026-08-30: this document used to warn that the Hydra must
	  never be caught by the blanket "OCB to 0" rule. What the code does is
	  the opposite, and it is deliberate. Type 63 is not in KEEP_OCB, so a
	  placed Hydra gets OCB 0 and stands as a single centre head; the pool
	  never moves a trio, because kHerds contains only the Compy. What the
	  code demands instead is space: kSpaceNeeds gives type 63 a 3x3 window
	  in which at least 5 of the 9 tiles must be free. Andrea2's three
	  vanilla Hydras are in fixedItems and are never touched, so the
	  original chain of three stays intact wherever it already is.

	  CORRECTION 2026-09-03: the threshold was all nine tiles until then,
	  and measured over twenty seeds that discarded 93 percent of the
	  placement spots - 0.65 Hydras per full run, not one of them through
	  the swap, and in the Andy levels practically never. The user set it
	  to 5 of 9 and gave up the arena idea behind the nine ("that was only
	  a rough guideline"). At the same time the space rule moved into the
	  swap's pre-filter: candidates are sifted BEFORE the roll, as the
	  height rule already was, so a place that could take some kind is no
	  longer left at vanilla just because the roll happened to hit the
	  Hydra.

	4 CHEF (chef.cpp, type 87) - NO LONGER A SPECIAL CASE. Fixed under #124
	  and now an ordinary pool enemy.
	  What was true and is worth keeping: ChefControl never read hit_points,
	  so weapon damage drained his health bar while his own logic ignored
	  it - he stayed active forever. The only real death ran through the
	  level-bound cutscene in deltapak.cpp (stealth3_start/end), which sets
	  hit_points, current_anim_state and anim_number by hand. Moved to
	  another level he was therefore unkillable, which is why he was
	  excluded.
	  What changed: ChefControl now opens with a hit_points <= 0 branch and
	  borrows the soldier family's gunshot death, his skeleton being
	  bone-identical with theirs. He is row 87 in the cross pool (donor
	  joby3, animCount 12, anim base 460), is offered in the GUI as "Chef",
	  and gets spawn OCB 1 so he hunts instead of chopping vegetables. Only
	  joby3's own item 186 stays vanilla, so the cutscene there still works.

	  This is worth remembering as a CLASS of bug, not one enemy: a control
	  routine that never reads hit_points makes its enemy immortal, and it
	  only shows once weapons are scattered where Core never put any. Three
	  were found this way - chef, skeleton, swamp witch. The check is one
	  grep: hit_points in the enemy's own .cpp.
