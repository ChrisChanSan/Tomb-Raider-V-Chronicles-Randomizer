Copy your HQ audio files from your own TR4-6 Remaster installation
into the folders of the languages you want to use with the ingame
language switch. Nothing of the Remaster is included here.

JP is still WIP.

The sound effects need no copy any more: with the TR4-6 Remaster
installed, the game reads its MAIN.SFX (it sits there in 5\DATA)
where it lies and plays its sound effects at 44.1 kHz instead of the
original 22 kHz. A MAIN.SFX in this folder, under that name, is used
before it - for those who copied one here already. With neither, the
game plays the original samples as it always did.
